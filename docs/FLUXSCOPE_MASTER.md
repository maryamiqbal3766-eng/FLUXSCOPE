# TADBIR — Master Product Definition

## Product Name

TADBIR — تدبیر

## Product Type

AI-Powered Economic Shock & Business Decision-Support System

---

## Core Product Statement

TADBIR helps a business understand how an external economic shock will affect its own operations and finances, then lets the owner test possible responses before deciding.

---

# Core Workflow

DETECT → TRACE → QUANTIFY → SIMULATE → COMPARE → RESPOND → MONITOR

---

# 1. DETECT — Identify the Economic Shock

TADBIR continuously retrieves relevant information from trusted/approved sources such as SBP, PBS and government/economic publications.

It identifies meaningful changes such as:

- Exchange-rate movement
- Fuel-price increase
- Electricity/energy-price change
- Interest-rate change
- Inflation/input-cost movement
- Import/export policy changes
- Major tax/duty changes

### AI Role

The AI interprets the external information and converts it into a structured Economic Shock Event.

Example:

USD/PKR changed significantly.

Instead of simply telling the user the news, TADBIR asks:

> "Could this affect your business?"

---

---

# 3. QUANTIFY — Calculate the Actual Impact

Once the relationship is identified, TADBIR's deterministic calculation engine calculates the estimated effect.

Example:

USD/PKR +7%

TADBIR could calculate:

- Imported input cost: +Rs. X
- Monthly total cost: +Rs. X
- Gross margin: X% → Y%
- Monthly cash requirement: +Rs. X
- Estimated profit impact: Rs. X

## Important Rule

AI does not invent the numbers.

The calculation engine performs the mathematics using:

- verified external data
- user/business data
- explicitly stated assumptions

---

# 4. SIMULATE — "What If I Respond Differently?"

This is the second major core feature.

The business owner can change assumptions.

Examples:

What if I increase prices by 4%?

Calculate.

What if I reduce imported material by 15%?

Calculate.

What if I absorb the additional cost?

Calculate.

What if the exchange rate increases another 5%?

Calculate.

TADBIR recalculates the business model for every scenario.

---

# 5. COMPARE — Put the Options Side by Side

TADBIR does not simply say:

> "Do X."

Instead, it produces a transparent comparison.

| Response | Cost Impact | Margin | Cash Requirement | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Increase price 4% | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| Reduce imports 15% | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| Absorb cost | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| Custom scenario | ... | ... | ... | ... |

The user can see the trade-offs.

---

# 6. RESPOND — Human Makes the Decision

This is an important TADBIR principle:

> AI informs. Human decides.

The system can generate a decision brief:

### Current situation

Exchange-rate shock is increasing imported input costs.

### Estimated exposure

X% of your COGS is affected.

### Scenario results

Scenario A → ...

Scenario B → ...

Scenario C → ...

Then:

**Choose / Modify / Create Your Own Response**

The business owner remains responsible for the decision.

---

# 7. MONITOR — Watch What Happens After the Decision

After the user chooses a response, TADBIR can track:

- Actual costs
- Sales
- Margins
- Exchange rate
- Energy prices
- Actual vs projected impact

Then identify:

Projected impact: Rs. X

Actual impact: Rs. Y

If conditions change:

> "The economic assumption has changed. Your previous plan may need to be recalculated."

This becomes the basis for the longer-term product.
